User guide

How to use Hong Kong Buddy in an activity

Use this guide before, during, and after a Cantonese practice or HKBU visit activity.

01

Before the visit

Choose your role, learn polite greetings, practise pronunciation, and check the privacy reminder.

  1. Pick International Student or Visit Translation mode.
  2. Practise short greetings with Jyutping and meaning.
  3. Do not record private or sensitive information.
02

During the visit

Use short sentences, confirm meaning, show large text when helpful, and ask the AI to simplify.

  1. Start with one greeting or comfort-check phrase.
  2. Type if the room is noisy or microphone use is not appropriate.
  3. Confirm important meanings with a human volunteer.
03

After the visit

Review useful phrases, report confusing AI output, and clear local practice notes when needed.

  1. Save only non-sensitive phrases you want to practise again.
  2. Use Correct Me to review your own Cantonese line.
  3. Tell staff if an answer felt wrong, unsafe, or culturally unclear.

About this app

Hong Kong Buddy is an AI Cantonese practice assistant for student life and supervised HKBU activity support. It is a learning and translation aid, not a replacement for staff judgement.

Privacy and AI limits

Microphone access is optional. Typed and spoken practice may be sent to configured AI providers to generate responses. Avoid names, addresses, medical details, student IDs, and other sensitive information.
